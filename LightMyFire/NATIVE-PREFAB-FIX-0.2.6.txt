LightMyFire 0.2.6 native prefab integration fix

- Replaces raw Unity Object.Instantiate cloning with Jotunn CustomPiece(name, baseName, PieceConfig), which routes through PrefabManager.CreateClonedPrefab / AssetManager.ClonePrefab and preserves Valheim soft-reference asset dependencies.
- Requires Piece + ZNetView + Container + WearNTear + native collider hierarchy before registering a base prefab.
- Validates the cloned custom barrel before PieceManager registration.
- Keeps the native barrel interaction, collider, networking and WearNTear component graph intact.
- Cosmetic lid/plaque are collider-free and cannot intercept clicks or damage.
- Accessory fitting now uses native non-trigger collider bounds, with active-renderer fallback, instead of all renderer bounds.
- Lid and plaque positions are expressed directly in native root-local coordinates.
- Strict Coal-only / Resin-only inventory filtering remains enabled.
- Build requirements remain explicit: 100 Wood, 40 Iron, 40 Tar.
- Reworked custom accessory textures toward darker, matte, lower-contrast stylized materials.
